# Board reference

[`board-picture.jpg`](./board-picture.jpg) is the visual reference for the normal-side Summer Pavilion player-board geometry.

The bonus definitions were cross-checked against the [official English rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-nextmove/uploads/sites/4/2024/06/EN-Azul-SP-Rules-2019-12-16_compressed.pdf).

Clockwise outer-star order, beginning at the upper-left, is orange, red, blue, yellow, green, purple. Every outer star is rotated so:

- costs 5-6 meet the outer window;
- costs 2-3 meet the radial pillar and center star;
- costs 1-2 meet the next clockwise statue;
- costs 3-4 meet the previous clockwise statue.

The center star remains numbered 1-6 clockwise from the top.
